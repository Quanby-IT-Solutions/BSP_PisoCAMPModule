# CE-1 neutral seed image provenance

Both PNGs were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool in its default mode, one call per final asset. No CLI or API fallback was used. The selected files were visually reviewed as text-free, clearly fictional educational illustrations and their selected bytes are frozen.

## Banknote education illustration

Final file: `source/neutral-banknote-education.png`

Prompt:

> Use case: scientific-educational
> Asset type: offline mobile learning illustration for a Philippine central-bank currency education module
> Primary request: create a neutral, clearly fictional educational illustration representing the concept of a paper banknote, without depicting or imitating any real Philippine or foreign banknote
> Scene/backdrop: clean warm off-white background with subtle soft geometric shapes
> Subject: one generic rectangular paper note shown at a gentle three-quarter angle, with abstract guilloche-inspired curves, simple geometric security-feature motifs, and a small magnifier accent; every motif must be obviously invented and non-functional
> Style/medium: polished flat editorial raster illustration with subtle paper texture and restrained depth, suitable for a trustworthy public education app
> Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, centered subject with generous safe margins for responsive cropping
> Lighting/mood: soft, clear, calm, educational
> Color palette: teal, muted blue, warm gold, and charcoal accents; do not match any specific circulating note
> Constraints: no readable text, no letters, no numbers, no currency symbols, no denomination, no portraits, no people, no seals, no flags, no logos, no signatures, no serial numbers, no watermark, no photorealistic currency, no claim of official artwork
> Avoid: replicas, recognizable currency layouts, real banknote landmarks, intricate anti-counterfeit detail that could be mistaken for a real note

## Coin education illustration

Final file: `source/neutral-coin-education.png`

Prompt:

> Use case: scientific-educational
> Asset type: offline mobile learning illustration for a Philippine central-bank currency education module
> Primary request: create a neutral, clearly fictional educational illustration representing the concept of coins, without depicting or imitating any real Philippine or foreign coin
> Scene/backdrop: clean warm off-white background with subtle soft geometric shapes
> Subject: a small arrangement of three generic round metal tokens at different angles, one face-up with abstract concentric geometry, one tilted to show a ridged edge, and a small magnifier accent; all face motifs must be obviously invented and non-functional
> Style/medium: polished flat editorial raster illustration with subtle metallic texture and restrained depth, suitable for a trustworthy public education app
> Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, centered group with generous safe margins for responsive cropping
> Lighting/mood: soft, clear, calm, educational
> Color palette: muted silver, warm brass, teal shadows, and charcoal accents; do not match any specific circulating coin
> Constraints: no readable text, no letters, no numbers, no currency symbols, no denomination, no portraits, no people, no seals, no flags, no logos, no signatures, no mint marks, no watermark, no photorealistic currency, no claim of official artwork
> Avoid: replicas, recognizable coin faces, national emblems, real coin layouts, intricate mint detail that could be mistaken for a real coin
