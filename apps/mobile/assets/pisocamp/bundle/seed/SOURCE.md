# CE-1 neutral seed image provenance

Both PNGs were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool in its default mode, one call per final asset. No CLI or API fallback was used. The selected files were visually reviewed as text-free, clearly fictional educational illustrations and their selected bytes are frozen.

## Banknote education illustration

Final file: `source/neutral-banknote-education.png`

Prompt:

> Use case: scientific-educational
> Asset type: offline mobile learning illustration for a Philippine central-bank currency education module
> Primary request: create a neutral, clearly fictional educational illustration representing the concept of a paper banknote, without depicting or imitating any real Philippine or foreign banknote
> Scene/backdrop: clean warm off-white background with subtle soft geometric shapes
> Subject: one generic rectangular paper note shown at a gentle three-quarter angle, with abstract guilloche-inspired curves, simple geometric security-feature motifs, and a small magnifier accent; every motif must be obviously invented and non-functional
> Style/medium: polished flat editorial raster illustration with subtle paper texture and restrained depth, suitable for a trustworthy public education app
> Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, centered subject with generous safe margins for responsive cropping
> Lighting/mood: soft, clear, calm, educational
> Color palette: teal, muted blue, warm gold, and charcoal accents; do not match any specific circulating note
> Constraints: no readable text, no letters, no numbers, no currency symbols, no denomination, no portraits, no people, no seals, no flags, no logos, no signatures, no serial numbers, no watermark, no photorealistic currency, no claim of official artwork
> Avoid: replicas, recognizable currency layouts, real banknote landmarks, intricate anti-counterfeit detail that could be mistaken for a real note

# CE-2 neutral coin model provenance

Final file: `source/neutral-coin-education.glb`

This model is not artwork and was not generated by an image or model tool. It is
produced procedurally by `packages/db/src/pisocamp-coin-model.ts`, which is its
only source; the checked-in `.glb` is a build artifact that
`pnpm --filter @repo/db seed:bundle:validate` reproduces byte for byte.

The geometry is a reeded cylinder with two abstract concentric-band faces, one
warm and one cool so the obverse and reverse are told apart at a glance, and an
alternating ridged edge. Every motif is invented and non-functional. The model
carries no readable text, letters, numerals, currency symbols, denominations,
portraits, people, emblems, seals, flags, logos, signatures or mint marks, and
no claim of official artwork. It represents the idea of a coin so that the 3D
viewer can be exercised for every mandatory denomination without reproducing,
imitating or implying any real circulating coin.

The generator calls no library trigonometric function: the circumference is
walked by repeated complex multiplication by a fixed step and renormalised with
a square root, both exactly specified by IEEE-754, so the bytes are identical on
every platform that runs it.

# Seed signing key

The packaged seed is signed with a throwaway Ed25519 key that is discarded
immediately after signing; only the raw public key and the signature are kept,
in `packages/db/src/pisocamp-seed-bundle.ts`. When the seed legitimately
changes, it is re-signed with a fresh throwaway key and both constants move in
the same diff. `ce2-seed-v2` supersedes `ce1-seed-v1`, which signed the seed
before the coin model was added.

# CE-4 tactile simulation map provenance

Source: `packages/db/src/pisocamp-tactile-catalog.ts`

The map is not artwork and was not generated by any tool. It is three named
rectangles, in permille of the packaged neutral banknote illustration, each
naming an approved pattern from the client's pattern catalog.

The regions are positions on that **fictional** illustration, not on any real
note. They teach what kinds of tactile feature a banknote carries and roughly
where one is found; they are not a guide to the layout of a specific
denomination, and the viewer states on screen that it is a simulation before
anything can be touched. Every mandatory banknote shares the map, exactly as
every mandatory coin shares one neutral 3D model: the illustration they are
anchored to is the same one.

The pattern catalog itself lives in the app, in
`apps/mobile/lib/features/pisocamp/data/tactile_patterns.dart`, because what a
device can play is a property of the device rather than of the content. A bundle
that named a pattern the app does not have would fail the coverage test rather
than silently do nothing.

`ce4-seed-v3` supersedes `ce2-seed-v2`, re-signed with a fresh throwaway key
when the tactile map joined the seed.
